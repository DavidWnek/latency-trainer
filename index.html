Peek Latency Trainer

Hold the angle. A peeker will swing the corner at a random moment — click or tap (or press Space) the instant he appears.

Times real round trips from this device. Open the app on your phone over Wi-Fi to see what Wi-Fi adds.

Ping is round-trip; each direction is half. The varied side uses the test value, the other side its base ping. Jitter adds a random 0–N ms to every packet; lost packets never arrive.

What the columns mean
Bot reaction / aim time
He notices you after his reaction time, then turns and raises his gun over the aim time before firing.
Median RT
Your reaction: click or tap time minus the first frame he was drawn.
Peek adv.
How long he could see you before you could see him.
Window
Longest reaction that still wins outright — your shot has to reach the server a tick before his. In Dodge: the latest you can start moving and still get off his screen before he fires.
Won
Outright wins, or escapes in Dodge. Trades and no-reactions count as losses.
Killed behind cover
Dodge only: your screen already had you fully behind the wall when the kill arrived. The server accepted his shot because his screen still showed you in the open.

The server is tick-based and lag-compensated: each shot is judged against what its shooter saw. The peeker's ping delays both his appearance on your screen and his shot by the same amount, so it mostly cancels. Your own ping hits you twice: once seeing him, once getting your shot back to the server. Display and input lag on your machine are not modelled.
